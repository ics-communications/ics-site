About these dates

  • Dates are subject to change. A date marked with an asterisk (*) is tentative.
  • JM deadlines apply to students; SM deadlines apply to instructors. TST dates are set by the Toronto School of Theology and can differ from ICS dates.
  • Questions about a deadline, or a date that looks wrong? Write to academic-registrar@icscanada.edu.
