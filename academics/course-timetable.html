About this timetable

  • Every course title links to the course catalogue, where you will find the full description, the required reading, prerequisites and the syllabus.
  • To register, or to ask whether a course still has room, write to academic-registrar@icscanada.edu. Registration deadlines for each term are on the Academic Calendar.
  • Times are Eastern. Courses listed here are offered subject to sufficient enrolment, and a seminar can be withdrawn or rescheduled.
  • A CSTC number after a title is the course's area within the Christian Educator Certificates.
