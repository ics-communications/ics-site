Before you apply

Three things to do, in order, and what each degree asks of you.

Deadline February 1 Applications for fall admission to the MA and PhD. See the academic calendar

Prepare

  1. 500–1,000words Statement of purpose: how study at ICS will fit with your academic goals. Document
  2. 10–20pages Academic writing sample, preferably a research paper. Document

Apply

  1. $90CAD Complete the form below and pay the application fee in Canadian dollars ($120 after the deadline). Paying sends your application. Fee

Then arrange

  1. Allinstitutions Official transcripts from every post-secondary institution you have attended, sent directly to academic-registrar@icscanada.edu. Transcripts
  2. 2 or 3letters Academic reference letters: two for the MA, three for the PhD, plus one personal referee. Referees write to the Registrar directly. References

Who can apply

MA
Four-year bachelor’s degree or equivalent, with a B+ average. A concentration related to your intended field is preferred.
PhD
Master’s degree in your intended field. A background in philosophy is encouraged, not required. No master’s thesis? Ask the Program Director about your preparation.
English
English proficiency is required. See the requirements for international students.
Outside Canada
Write to the Academic Registrar about studying from outside Canada.
