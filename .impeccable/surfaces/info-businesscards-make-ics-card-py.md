# ICS business card (print)

Scope: the 3.5 × 2 in staff business card. Front and back, with 0.125 in bleed. Delivered as a fillable PDF template plus a batch generator (`info/BusinessCards/make_ics_card.py`). Visitor mode: Persuade, since the card is the institution in someone's hand.

Audience: people an ICS faculty or staff member meets: prospective students, donors, colleagues, church partners. Job: remember who they met, and reach them by email or phone.

Constraints: standard full-colour digital print. Fields: name, credentials/pronouns (optional), two title lines, email, phone. Fixed: icscanada.edu, the institution name, and a one-line address. The old card's three-line address block is not carried over. The logo is used intact; the dove form may also appear as a tone-on-tone watermark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The card speaks the ICS tagline as call and response. The front opens with "a tradition of inquiry," and the back answers "a spirit of engagement." Flipping the card completes the sentence. It refuses the stock layout of a logo-left lockup beside a stacked contact block.

OWN-WORLD: The website's world. Cream #FDFBF7 front, wine #9B0D24 drenched back, copper #A85A32 for the voice, ink #2E1318. Libre Baskerville carries the name and the two halves of the tagline (italic). Outfit carries the facts. A single copper hairline. The dove C sits tone-on-tone in wine-deep, oversized and bleeding off the back's right edge.

STORY: The recipient reads the opening phrase and the name. The hanging comma prompts them to turn the card, and the back resolves the sentence, then names the institution and its address.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Front. Copper italic call at top left. Name in wine Baskerville at about 15 pt in the upper-middle, with credentials, then two title lines. A short copper hairline. Email, phone and web at the bottom left. The red ICS logo at the bottom right, its foot on the web baseline. Back: full wine; the answer in cream italic at about 16 pt in the upper left; the giant dove watermark at the right; the white logo, name and one-line address along the bottom.

FORM: Call and Response, dealt lead (index 7 of 7 grounded), seed key fd785db1.
