Why join?

Membership at ICS

ICS membership will ensure that you will be invited into important conversations concerning the future of this cherished institution, as well as opportunities to discuss pressing cultural, spiritual, and political topics.

Specifically, you will hear from us, both Senior and Junior Members, through Perspective, Ground Motive, and Critical Faith. Moreover, you will be invited to participate in our workshops, symposia, and community conferences, and you can make your voice heard at the Annual General Meeting in December.
