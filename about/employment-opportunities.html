About the Institute

ICS brings a rich tradition of critical inquiry to the issues shaping our complex world.

The Institute for Christian Studies (ICS) is an accredited, independent graduate school of interdisciplinary philosophy and theology, affiliated with the Toronto School of Theology at the University of Toronto. ICS brings a rich tradition of critical inquiry to the issues shaping our complex world. We are equipping new generations of scholars and leaders to ask the difficult questions and challenge the easy answers. Our MA and PhD graduates are providing leadership across the globe — in universities, churches, and social agencies.
