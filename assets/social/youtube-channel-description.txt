YOUTUBE CHANNEL DESCRIPTION — RECOMMENDED
==========================================

Lectures, conversations, and public talks from the Institute for Christian Studies (ICS), a graduate school of philosophy in Toronto, Canada.

Since 1967, ICS has been a place where faith and scholarship meet. Rooted in the reformational Christian tradition, we offer MA and PhD programs in philosophy and a Master of Worldview Studies. Our faculty and guests take on questions about art, politics, religion, education, and what it means to live wisely in today's world.

On this channel you'll find recorded lectures, faculty interviews, conference sessions, and events open to the public. Subscribe to get new videos as they're posted.

Learn more and get in touch: icscanada.edu

(We don't monitor YouTube comments or messages. For questions, please use the contact information on our website.)
