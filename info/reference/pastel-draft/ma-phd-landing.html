Our Approach

Where Rigorous Philosophy Meets Radical Faith

ICS is not a typical graduate school. Rooted in the Reformational tradition, we cultivate scholars who ask fundamental questions about God, creation, and culture — and who find in Christian faith not a boundary to inquiry, but its very wellspring. Here, philosophy is always more than academic exercise: it is a practice of wisdom for the whole of life.

Fields of Study

Seven Interdisciplinary Neighbourhoods

Unlike programs organized around philosophy's sub-disciplines, ICS gathers courses into overlapping fields of study — thematic neighbourhoods that invite you to find your own pathway of inquiry.

Aesthetics, Hermeneutics & Philosophy of Discourse

Beauty, interpretation, and the power of language — exploring how meaning is made and communicated across culture.

Education, Epistemology & Metaphysics

The nature of knowledge and reality — and how these fundamental questions shape teaching, learning, and human formation.

Faith & Philosophy

The encounter between reason and revelation — examining how faith and philosophical inquiry illumine and challenge one another.

Politics, Society & Civil Life

Democracy, pluralism, justice, and the common good — thinking Christianly about our shared public life and institutions.

Religious Studies

Secularization, religious traditions, practices in the public sphere, and the development of religious language and thought.

Theological Studies

Christology, eschatology, theological aesthetics, spirituality, and the intersection of faith and culture in historic and contemporary thought.

The ICS Difference

Scholarship as a Practice of Wisdom

ICS understands itself as a community of scholars for whom the gospel's message of renewal shapes our collaborative pursuit of wisdom. We call our students "Junior Members" and our faculty "Senior Members" — because here, learning is apprenticeship, not transaction.

  • Reformational Tradition — Rooted in the thought of Dooyeweerd, Vollenhoven, and the Amsterdam School, reimagined for today's world
  • Interdisciplinary by Design — Courses draw from theology, aesthetics, politics, hermeneutics, and continental philosophy
  • Intimate Community — Small seminars and close faculty mentorship make ICS unlike any other graduate school
  • Toronto School of Theology — Affiliated with the TST, connecting you to a broader academic community in one of the world's great university cities

Why ICS

A Different Kind of Graduate School

Integral Scholarship

At ICS, Christian faith isn't applied to scholarship — it shapes it from the ground up. Every course and seminar is an exercise in thinking Christianly about the whole of life.

Mentored Learning

With small classes and close faculty mentorship, ICS offers something rare: a truly personal apprenticeship in Christian philosophical thought.

Global Reach

Located in Toronto and affiliated with the TST, ICS connects you to a worldwide network of scholars. The PhD can be pursued conjointly with the Free University of Amsterdam.

Community Supported

ICS is sustained by a community of supporters who believe in the mission of Christian scholarship. This means generous financial aid and accessible tuition.

Whether you're drawn to the MA or the PhD, ICS invites you into a community where rigorous thought and living faith meet. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
