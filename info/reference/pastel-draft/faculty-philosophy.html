Dr. Nik Ansell

Associate Professor of Theology

PhD (VU University, Amsterdam) · MPhil (Institute for Christian Studies) · BA (University of Bristol)

Nik’s Vision
Theology includes reflection on key meta-theological issues that frame the discipline—how the sacred is related to life as a whole, as the hope or heartbeat of the universe, as the revelation of God, self, world, and other. My teaching and research focus on systematic and biblical theology, notably Christology, eschatology, Old Testament wisdom thinking, and the theology of gender.

Courses Taught

  • Biblical Foundations: Narrative, Wisdom, and the Art of Reading Scripture

Research Interests

  • Christology & Eschatology
  • Old Testament Wisdom Thinking
  • Theology of Gender
  • Phenomenology of Revelation
  • Covenantal Participation & Imaging the Divine

Selected Publications

  • Ansell, N. (2013). The Annihilation of Hell: Universal Salvation and the Redemption of Time in the Eschatology of Jürgen Moltmann. Wipf & Stock.
  • Ansell, N. (1994). The Woman Will Overcome The Warrior: A Dialogue with the Christian/Feminist Theology of Rosemary Radford Ruether. University Press of America.
  • Ansell, N. (2001). The Call of Wisdom/The Voice of the Serpent: A Canonical Approach to the Tree of Knowledge. Christian Scholar’s Review, 31(1), 31–58.

Dr. Neal DeRoo

Professor of Philosophy

PhD (Boston College) · MA (Institute for Christian Studies) · BA (Calvin College)

Neal’s Vision
My work tries to account for how spirituality is constantly at work in constituting our experience and the implications that has for how we live our lives. I speak to academic, popular, and professional audiences—showing that the deep-seated spiritual forces shaping our experience help us think about faith as something that impacts our everyday life together.

Courses Taught

  • Philosophical Foundations: Movements, Theories, and Methods
  • What is Religion?
  • Philosophies of Oppression
  • What’s Christian About Christian Education?

Research Interests

  • Phenomenology (Husserl, Merleau-Ponty, Derrida, Levinas)
  • Critical Phenomenology
  • Philosophy of Religion
  • Relationship between God & Materiality
  • Philosophies of Liturgy

Selected Publications

  • DeRoo, N. (2022). The Political Logic of Experience: Expression in Phenomenology. Fordham University Press.
  • DeRoo, N. (2013). Futurity in Phenomenology: Promise and Method in Husserl, Levinas, and Derrida. Fordham University Press.
  • DeRoo, N., Simmons, J.A., & Benson, B.E. (Eds.) (2023). Philosophies of Liturgy: Explorations of Embodied Religious Practice. Bloomsbury.

Dr. Ronald A. Kuipers

Professor of Philosophy of Religion

President of ICS · Director, Centre for Philosophy, Religion & Social Ethics

PhD (VU University, Amsterdam) · MPhil (Institute for Christian Studies) · BA (The King’s University)

Ron’s Vision
Christianity, if it is anything at all, is a holistic pattern of living, and not simply a matter of intellectual believing. My work in the philosophy of religion takes up Wittgenstein’s challenge to speak an old language that yet belongs to a newer world, hoping to retrieve redemptive possibilities for our current existence that our present culture has trouble envisioning.

Courses Taught

  • Spiritual Foundations: Faith, Hope, and Life Together
  • Religion, Secularization & Democracy
  • Pragmatism, Race & Religion

Research Interests

  • Religion in the Public Sphere
  • Faith & Reason
  • Meaning of Religious Language
  • Religion, Secularization & Democracy
  • American Pragmatism & Critical Theory

Selected Publications

  • Kuipers, R.A. (2013). Richard Rorty. Bloomsbury (Contemporary American Thinkers series).
  • Kuipers, R.A. (2002). Critical Faith: Toward a Renewed Understanding of Religious Life and its Public Accountability. Rodopi.
  • Dudiak, J., Kuipers, R.A., & Sweetman, R. (Eds.) (2022). Post-Truth? Facts and Faithfulness.

Dr. Rebekah Smick

Associate Professor of Philosophy of Arts and Culture

PhD (University of Toronto) · MA (Columbia University) · BA (Brandeis University)

Rebekah’s Vision
In the hope of better understanding our contemporary valuation of the arts and their relation to theology and religion, unearthing the intricacies of the relationship between art and religion as they get expressed in the art literature of the early modern period is what orients my teaching and research.

Research Interests

  • Philosophy of Art
  • Theology of Art
  • Rhetoric & Poetics
  • History of Art
  • Aesthetics of Compassion

Selected Publications

  • Smick, R. (2021). Kuyper’s Calvin: The Aesthetics of Disenchantment. In Seeking Stillness or The Sound of Wings. Wipf & Stock.
  • Smick, R. (2016). “The Second Book of God”: Protestant Mysticism. In Mystical Landscapes: from Vincent van Gogh to Emily Carr. DelMonico Books–Prestel. (Winner, Canadian Museum’s Association Research Award, 2017.)

Dr. Michael DeMoor

Associate Professor of Social Philosophy

The King’s University, Edmonton · Cross-Appointed to ICS

PhD (VU University, Amsterdam) · MPhilF (Institute for Christian Studies) · BA

Michael’s Vision
My research focuses on how different conceptions of human rationality do and should shape how we make and justify public policy. I bring questions from political philosophy, the history of political thought, and Reformational social philosophy to bear on issues of democracy, human rights, and the common good.

King’s University Profile

Dr. Jeffrey Dudiak

Professor of Philosophy

The King’s University, Edmonton · Cross-Appointed to ICS

PhD · MPhilF (Institute for Christian Studies) · MA · BA

Jeff’s Vision
My work explores how human beings can live more peacefully with one another by understanding the conditions of possibility for dialogue. Drawing on Levinas, I seek to show that dialogue is ultimately possible only for a gracious subjectivity already graced by God by way of the other—and that peace comes down to the subject’s willingness to take the beautiful risk of a peaceful gesture.

Research Interests

  • Emmanuel Levinas & Ethics
  • Philosophy of Dialogue & Peace
  • Continental Philosophy
  • Philosophy of Religion

Selected Publications

  • Dudiak, J. (2001). The Intrigue of Ethics: A Reading of the Idea of Discourse in the Thought of Emmanuel Levinas. Fordham University Press.
  • Dudiak, J. Peace as Being Taught: The Philosophical Foundations of a Culture of Peace. In Egéa-Kuehne, D. (Ed.), Levinas and Education. Routledge.
  • Dudiak, J. (2004). Religion with an Impure Heart: Levinas on Kierkegaardian Religion. In Smith, J.K.A. & Venema, H. (Eds.), The Hermeneutics of Charity. Brazos Press, 185–196.
