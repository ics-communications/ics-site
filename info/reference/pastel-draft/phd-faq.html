Everything you need to know about the PhD — from the two degree tracks to examinations, timelines, and financial support.

Program Degree Tracks Admissions Exams & Dissertation Costs & Aid

Program Overview

The PhD is intended for Junior Members whose principal vocation is the life of scholarship and teaching. It is a mixed program with coursework, a pre-dissertation examination, and an original dissertation. Graduates are well-equipped for research or teaching positions worldwide.

Expect approximately two years of full-time coursework followed by three years of dissertation preparation, writing, and defense — roughly five years total. Individual timelines vary based on your research and field.

The same seven interdisciplinary fields as the MA: Aesthetics, Hermeneutics & Philosophy of Discourse; Anthropology & Ethics; History & Historiography of Philosophy; Knowledge, Truth & Learning; Philosophy of Religion & Philosophical Theology; Social & Political Philosophy; and Theology & Biblical Studies.

Degree Tracks

Both tracks share the same coursework and examination structure. The ICS Standalone PhD is granted by ICS and defended in Toronto. The Conjoint PhD with VU Amsterdam involves course work and exams at ICS, with the defense and degree granting in Amsterdam. Choose the track that best fits your career goals.

No. All coursework and exams are completed at ICS in Toronto. You travel to Amsterdam only for the final dissertation defense.

Admissions

A master’s degree including a thesis component in the area of intended study, and a demonstrable background in philosophy. Proficiency in English is required.

The application deadline for fall PhD admission is February 1. Late applications may be considered on a case-by-case basis but may not be eligible for financial aid.

The PhD program is not offered for part-time studies. Full-time commitment is required for the coursework phase.

Yes. ICS welcomes international applicants. English proficiency is required (TOEFL iBT 100+ or IELTS Academic 7.0+). ICS is a designated educational institution for Canadian study permits.

Exams & Dissertation

A rigorous assessment conducted by a panel of internal and external examiners. It tests your mastery of relevant works from your study at ICS and includes both written and oral components. Passing leads to a formal dissertation proposal.

Two foreign languages relevant to your dissertation area. Reading knowledge must be demonstrated before advancement to candidacy.

ICS Standalone PhD candidates defend in Toronto. Conjoint PhD candidates defend in Amsterdam at the Free University.

Costs & Financial Aid

Tuition and fees are listed on the ICS tuition page. As a community-supported institution, ICS provides generous financial aid. Contact the Academic Registrar for current figures.

Financial aid availability applies to both tracks. Discuss your specific situation with the Academic Registrar for details on funding your Amsterdam defense travel.
