1967–2001

Hendrik Hart† (deceased)

PhD

  • Systematic Philosophy

The Institute’s first professor, who taught systematic philosophy from its founding year until 2001. Understanding Our World: An Integral Ontology (1984) set out the categorial framework much of ICS’s later work argued with and built on.

Full profile

1968–2004

James Olthuis

PhD

  • Philosophical Theology

Joined the faculty in 1968 and has practised as a psychotherapist since 1982. In I Pledge You My Troth and The Beautiful Risk he trades mastery for love, and reads relationship itself as the site of healing.

Full profile

1972–1995

Calvin Seerveld† (deceased)

PhD

  • Aesthetics

A pioneering voice in Reformational aesthetics. Rainbows for the Fallen World (1980) gave a generation of Christian artists a way to square their faith with their vocation, and is still the book people arrive at ICS having read.
